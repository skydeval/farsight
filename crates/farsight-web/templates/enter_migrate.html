This version of Farsight signs the admin in with an ATProto account instead of a password. Enter the existing admin password one last time and the DID of the account that will administer this instance. After that the password no longer works, and you sign in at this address with that account.

The ATProto account that administers this instance: did:plc:… or did:web:…, not a handle. Farsight does not check that the account is yours; a wrong DID is corrected with farsight set-admin-did in the container.
