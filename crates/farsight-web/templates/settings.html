Public UI

A public lookup site under /public: anyone can see who blocks an account, directly and through lists. Removed records are never shown there; they are on the history pages reached from the DID and list lookups, after login. Every setting here applies on save, without a restart; a cache in front of Farsight keeps serving its copies for up to a minute. Resting the pointer on an account opens a card with its handle, DID and creation date. With this on, the card also has the account's picture, and the visitor's browser fetches it from the account's own server, whose operator then sees the visitor's address and the time. On a page listing who blocks an account, a blocker who runs their own server can log who looks. Off: cards keep everything else, and visitors' browsers contact only this instance.
