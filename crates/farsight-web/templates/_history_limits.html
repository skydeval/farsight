Limits

  • A block or membership that began and ended before this instance stored it is not here. This is most of what is missing.
  • Only lists that someone blocks are indexed; membership of other lists is never recorded. When a list stops being blocked its members are dropped without a record.
  • A removal this instance missed is recorded when it is noticed later, with the time it was noticed. Times stand still while the instance is disconnected from the network.
  • "First seen" is when this instance stored the record. It restarts when a list is indexed again.
  • Records of accounts that were deleted are removed, and accounts that are not active are not shown.
  • An account or list that removes very many records in a day has the excess left out.
  • A deleted list's members are recorded only up to that daily limit; a very large list is stored only in part.
  • The two sections are separate records. They can show that an account stopped blocking a list, and that someone was removed from a list, not that a given block covered a given account at a given time.
