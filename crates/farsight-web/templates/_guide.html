Blocked By
Accounts that block the account you looked up, one row for each block, newest first.
Blocked By Lists
Lists the account has been put on that other people subscribe to as block lists. The number at the top adds up those subscriptions.
Blocking
Accounts the account itself blocks, where this instance shows them.
History
The handles and hosts the account has had, as the PLC directory records them.
Members and Subscribers
On a list's page: who is on the list, and who subscribes to it as a block list. Mute subscriptions are private, so they are never shown.
suspended and taken down
What an account's host has done to it. Suspended accounts stay in the tables with their tag. Taken-down accounts are left out until you tick “Show taken down accounts”. Deactivated and deleted accounts are not shown.
Still being verified
A row appears only once this instance has checked that the handle belongs to the account, which takes a few seconds the first time an account is seen.
